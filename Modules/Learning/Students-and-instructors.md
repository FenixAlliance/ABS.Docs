# Students, instructors and enrollments

Set up the people who teach and take your courses, enroll students, and follow their enrollments.

## Who it is for

Course administrators and instructors who manage who teaches a course and who takes it.

## Availability

You need the course permissions to see and change students, instructors and enrollments. Students and instructors are in the Learning module, under **People**. Enrollments, cohorts and the course team are inside each course, under **People** in the course navigation.

## Students and instructors

Each student and each instructor has a profile in your workspace. A profile is linked to a contact, so the person's details stay in one place.

### Add a student or an instructor

1. In the Learning module, under **People**, select **Students** or **Instructors**.
2. Select **Add**. The **Create Student Profile** or **Create Instructor Profile** dialog opens.
3. Choose the **Contact**. You can also add an **About** text and an **Avatar URL**.
4. Select **Create**.

You do not need to create a student profile for a buyer. When someone buys a course and has no student profile yet, one is created for them.

## Assign instructors to a course

1. Open the course and, under **People**, select **Teams**.
2. Select **Add**. The **Add Team Member** dialog opens.
3. Choose the **Instructor** and their **Role**.
4. Select **Create**.

## Enroll a student

1. Open the course and, under **People**, select **Enrollments**.
2. Select **Add**. The **Create Enrollment** dialog opens.
3. Choose the **Student Profile**. You can also place the student in a **Cohort**.
4. Select **Create**.

An enrollment you create yourself is not tied to a sale, so a refund never ends it. To move a student to another cohort, select **Edit** on the enrollment.

The **Enrollments** list shows each enrollment's student, cohort and **Status**.

## Cohorts

A cohort is a group of students who take a course together, with its own **Start Date** and **End Date**. To create one, open the course, select **Cohorts** under **People**, select **Add**, enter a **Name** and the dates, and select **Create**.

## Enrollment status

Every enrollment has a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Active | The learner has access to the course. New enrollments start as active. |
| Completed | Coming. Today no enrollment reaches this status: enrollments are Active or Revoked. |
| Revoked | Access was withdrawn, for example after a full refund. |

A revoked enrollment is final. It is never reactivated. If the learner joins the course again, they get a new enrollment.

## Completion certificates

1. Open the course and, under **Assessments**, select **Certificate Templates** to prepare the template for the course. You can edit a template later. If you have a social profile, you are recorded as the template's author.
2. Select **Certificates**, then select **Add**. The **Create Certificate** dialog opens.
3. Choose the **Student Profile**, the **Enrollment** and the **Certificate Template**. The lists show each template by its title, and each enrollment by the student's name if you can read student profiles.
4. Select **Create**.

Staff issue certificates. A certificate stays valid after a refund. Learners see only their own certificates; staff with the course permissions see all of them.

Coming: certificates issued automatically when a learner completes a course.

## What learners see

Learners use the **Academy** area of their personal Studio menu.

- **My Courses**, on the student dashboard, lists the courses they are enrolled in. A course whose enrollment is revoked no longer appears.
- **Certificates** lists the completion certificates they hold.
- The student dashboard shows **Not available yet** for learning hours and pending tasks. Coming: progress, hours and task tracking.

Instructors see **Earnings** and **Payouts** pages in the Academy area. Coming: both are empty today and will show course sales once instructor earnings are tracked.
