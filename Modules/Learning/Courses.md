# Create and publish a course

Create a course in Studio, add its content, and publish it so learners can find it.

## Who it is for

Course authors and administrators who prepare courses for their workspace.

## Availability

You need permission to create and update courses. Courses are in the Learning module, under **Courses** > **All Courses**.

## Create a course

1. In Studio, open your workspace, go to **Modules**, and open the Learning module.
2. Under **Courses**, select **All Courses**.
3. Select **Add**. The **Create Course** dialog opens.
4. Enter a **Title** and a **Description**. Both are required. You can also fill in:
   - **Summary**, a short version of the description.
   - **Code**, your own reference for the course.
   - **Category** and **Instructor**.
   - **SKU** and **Version**.
   - **Start Date** and **End Date**, and **Inscriptions Start** and **Inscriptions End** for the enrollment period.
   - **Max Enrollments**.
   - **Total Effort (Weeks)**, **Hours Per Week** and **Total Effort (Hours)**.
5. Select **Create**.

A new course is not published. Learners do not see it in the public course catalog until you publish it.

## Add content

Open the course from the list with **View**. The course navigation on the left groups everything that belongs to the course.

Under **Content**:

- **Sections** and **Units** give the course its structure. A section holds units, and **Unit Components** are the building blocks inside a unit.
- **Wikis** and **Articles** hold reference material.
- **Pages** are web pages for the course. Course pages that use code are available to enabled workspaces.
- **Libraries**, **Handouts** and **Files** hold the material learners download or read.

Under **Assessments**:

- **Assignments** set work for learners, and **Assignment Types** describe the kinds of work you set.
- **Certificates** and **Certificate Templates** let you issue completion certificates.

Under **Communication**:

- **Updates** carry your announcements.
- **Forums** give the course community a place to talk.

## Publish the course

1. Open the course with **View**. The **Course Details** card opens on the **Overview**.
2. Select **Published**.
3. Select **Save**.

Published courses appear in the public course catalog. The public catalog shows only published courses.

## Edit or delete a course

- To change a course, open it with **View**, change its details and select **Save**. You can also select **Edit** from the course list.
- To delete a course from the course list, select **Delete** and confirm.
- To delete a course from its details, select **Delete**. The course is deleted at once, without a confirmation step.
- Deleting a course cannot be undone.
