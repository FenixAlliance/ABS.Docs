# Deal unit flows

A deal unit flow is the ordered set of stages your deals move through, such as qualification, proposal and negotiation.

**Who it is for:** sales managers who define how the team works its pipeline, and sellers who keep each deal at the right stage.

**Availability:** part of Sales Hub in every workspace. You need permission to manage deal units. In Studio, open your workspace, select **Modules**, open **Sales**, and select **Deal Unit Flows**.

## How flows work

- A flow has a name, a description and a list of stages.
- Each stage has a name, a number that sets its position in the flow, and a description.
- Each deal unit can follow one flow. You choose its **Flow** and its current **Flow Stage** on the deal unit, and you move it to the next stage yourself as the deal progresses.
- You can keep several flows, for example one for new customers and one for renewals.

## Create a flow

1. On the **Deal Unit Flows** page, select **Add**.
2. In **Create Deal Unit Flow**, enter a **Name** and a **Description**.
3. Select **Create Flow**.

## Add stages to a flow

1. In the flows grid, open the flow you created.
2. In the stages list, select **Add**.
3. In **Create Deal Unit Flow Stage**, enter a **Name**, an **Order** (1 for the first stage, 2 for the next, and so on) and a **Description**.
4. Select **Create Stage**.
5. Repeat for each stage.

To change a stage, select **Edit** on it. To remove one, select **Delete**.

## Change or delete a flow

1. Open the flow from the **Deal Unit Flows** page.
2. Under **Edit Deal Unit Flow**, change the **Name** or **Description** and select **Save Changes**.
3. To remove the flow, select **Delete** and confirm. Deleting a flow cannot be undone.

## Put a deal unit on a flow

1. Open the deal unit, or create one from the **Deal Units** page.
2. Choose the **Flow**, then the **Flow Stage**.
3. Save the deal unit.

See [Deal units](Sales-Records/Deal-Units.md) for the full steps.
