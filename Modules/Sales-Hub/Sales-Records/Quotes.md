# Quotes

A quote offers your prices to a customer for a set period, and your customer can turn it into an order while it is valid.

**Who it is for:** sellers who prepare and send quotes, sales managers who follow them up, and customers who receive them.

**Availability:** part of Sales Hub in every workspace. Viewing, creating, updating and deleting quotes are granted separately by role. In Studio, open your workspace, select **Modules**, open **Sales**, and select **Quotes**. Customers open the quotes they receive in their **Wallet**.

## The quote lifecycle

1. **You create the quote**, add its lines and set how long it is valid.
2. **You send it** by email, or share it.
3. **Your customer places the order** from the quote while it is open and not expired. The quote becomes **Accepted**, and the order appears in your **Orders** with the next number in your series.
4. **You close the quote** when it no longer applies. A closed quote cannot be ordered. You can reopen it if you need to.

A quote's status is **New**, **Accepted**, **Declined** or **Expired**.

## Create a quote

1. On the **Quotes** page, select **Add**.
2. In **Create Quote**, enter a **Title**.
3. Choose the customer's **Individual**, **Organization**, or both.
4. Choose a **Price List**.
5. To link the quote to an opportunity, choose it in **Deal Unit**.
6. To let your customer place the order from their own workspace, choose that workspace in **Receiver Tenant**.
7. Add a **Description** if you need one.
8. Select **Add Line**, choose the **Item** and enter the quantity. Repeat for each item.
9. Select **Create**.

A new quote is valid for one month by default.

## Set the validity period

1. Open the quote from the **Quotes** grid with **View**.
2. Under **Quote Details**, set **Valid From** and **Valid To**.
3. Select **Save Changes**.

After the **Valid To** date passes, your customer can no longer place an order from the quote.

## Work a quote

On the quote's page you can:

- **Manage its lines** on the **Products** tab, and its totals and **Payment Terms** on the **Totals** tab.
- **Set billing and shipping terms:** **Billing Location**, **Freight Terms**, **Shipping Location** and **Shipping Method**.
- **Recalculate** the totals after a change: open the actions menu and select **Recalculate Quote**.
- **Preview** what your customer sees: select **Preview Quote**.
- **See its orders** on the **Orders** tab.

## Send a quote

1. Open the quote.
2. In the actions menu, select **Send Email**.
3. Check the email preview, enter the recipient's **Email**, and select **Submit**.

The customer receives the quote with a link to view it. Only the people a quote was sent to can view it.

To share the quote another way, select **Share Quote**. You can share its link or create a QR code for it.

## How your customer places the order

Your customer does this in their own Studio, in the workspace you named as the quote's receiver.

1. In their **Wallet**, they open **Quotes** and select your quote.
2. They select **Place Order**.

The order takes the quote's lines, totals and currency. The quote becomes **Accepted**. Once the order exists, the button changes to **View Order**, so a quote is ordered only once.

A quote addressed to a person's own account, rather than to a workspace, appears in that person's personal wallet, where they place the order the same way.

The order cannot be placed when the quote is closed, has expired, or was already declined.

## Close or reopen a quote

1. Open the quote.
2. In the actions menu, select **Close Quote** and confirm with **Close**.

A closed quote cannot be changed or ordered. To reopen it, select **Reopen Quote**.

## Delete a quote

In the actions menu, select **Delete Quote** and confirm. The quote's lines are deleted with it. You cannot delete a quote that has invoices, payments, shipments or other related records. Deleting cannot be undone.
