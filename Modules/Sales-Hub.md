# Sales Hub

Sales Hub is where your sales team tracks opportunities, sends quotes and manages orders, from the first conversation to the invoice.

**Who it is for:** sales managers, account executives and the people who prepare quotes and process orders.

**Availability:** Sales Hub is part of every workspace. What each person can see and do depends on their role: viewing, creating, updating and deleting are granted separately for deal units, quotes, orders, price lists and loyalty programs. In Studio, open your workspace, select **Modules**, and open **Sales**.

## What you can do

- **Track opportunities** with [deal units](Sales-Hub/Sales-Records/Deal-Units.md). Add the products you expect to sell, see the calculated totals, and close each deal as won or lost.
- **Define your sales stages** with [deal unit flows](Sales-Hub/Sales-Flows.md), and place each deal unit at a stage.
- **Send quotes** with [quotes](Sales-Hub/Sales-Records/Quotes.md). Email a quote, set how long it is valid, and let your customer place the order from it.
- **Manage orders** with [orders](Sales-Hub/Sales-Records/Orders.md). Every order gets a number in your own series, for example ORD-000123, that cannot be edited.
- **Set your prices** with price lists. A price list holds the price of each item in a currency and unit, and you choose a price list on each deal unit, quote and order.
- **Keep loyalty program records.** A loyalty program record names the program and the price list that goes with it. It does not track points or rewards.
- **Keep sales literature** in one place for your team.
- **Bill your orders** with [invoices](~/Modules/Accounting/Billing/Billing-Records/Invoices.md), which your customer pays through the invoice's payment checkout.

## The Sales Hub home page

The **Sales Hub Module** home page links to each area: **Price Lists**, **Deal Units**, **Deal Unit Flows**, **Quotes**, **Orders**, **Invoices**, **Sales Literature** and **Loyalty Programs**. It also shows status cards for leads, orders, deal units and invoices, with outlook charts for orders and deal units.

## How the records fit together

A typical sale moves from a deal unit to a quote, from the quote to an order, and from the order to an invoice. Each step is optional: you can create a quote or an order directly. [Sales records](Sales-Hub/Sales-Records.md) explains how the records link to each other and to your contacts.

## Price lists

Use a price list to set the price your customers pay for each item.

1. On the Sales Hub home page, select **Price Lists**.
2. In the price lists grid, select **Add**. The **Create Price List** page opens with the form in a dialog.
3. Enter a **Name** and a **Description**, choose a **Currency**, and optionally a **Unit Group** and **Unit**.
4. Select **Create Price List**. The new price list opens.
5. Under **Create Price**, choose the **Item**, enter the **Price**, choose its **Currency** and, if you sell the item in units, its **Unit Group** and **Default Unit**.
6. Select **Save**. The price appears under **Existing Prices**. Repeat for each item.

To change a price, select it under **Existing Prices**, edit it and select **Save**.

## Loyalty programs

1. On the Sales Hub home page, select **Loyalty Programs**.
2. Select **Add**.
3. Enter a **Title**, choose a **Price List** and add a **Description**.
4. Select **Create**.
