# Orders

Orders are managed in Sales Hub, not in Billing.

An order records what a customer ordered from you. When a buyer checks out an order, the platform issues the invoice for it, and that invoice then appears under **Modules** > **Accounting** > **Billing** > **Invoices**.

To create and manage orders, see [Orders in Sales Hub](~/Modules/Sales-Hub/Sales-Records/Orders.md).
