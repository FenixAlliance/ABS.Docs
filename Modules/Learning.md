# Learning

The Learning module lets you build courses, run them for your learners, and sell them as part of your catalog.

## Who it is for

- Training teams, academies and schools that teach online.
- Businesses that sell courses alongside their other products and services.
- Instructors who prepare course content and follow their learners.

## Availability

The Learning module appears in Studio as **Learning Manager** in your workspace's modules. Open your workspace in Studio, go to **Modules**, and open the Learning module. Its menu has three groups:

- **Courses**: **All Courses**.
- **People**: **Students** and **Instructors**.
- **Catalog**: **Collections** and **Categories**.

To see courses you need permission to read courses. To add, edit or delete them you need the matching course permissions. Your workspace administrator grants these through security roles. Availability of the module can depend on your plan.

## What you can do

### Build courses

A course has a title, a code, a description, a schedule and an effort estimate. You organize its content in sections and units, and you add pages, wikis and articles, libraries, handouts and files. Each course has its own navigation in Studio, so everything that belongs to a course is in one place.

See [Create and publish a course](Learning/Courses.md).

### Organize your catalog

- **Categories** group courses by subject.
- **Collections** group related courses under one name, for example a learning path.

### Manage instructors and students

Instructors and students each have a profile linked to a contact in your workspace. You assign instructors to a course team, enroll students in a course, and organize enrolled students in cohorts.

See [Students, instructors and enrollments](Learning/Students-and-instructors.md).

### Sell courses

A course is part of your catalog, so you can sell it like any other item. When the buyer pays, they are enrolled in the course. A full refund ends the enrollment. To give a learner a course at no cost, enroll them yourself.

See [Sell courses](Learning/Selling-courses.md).

### Recognize completion

You can design certificate templates for a course and issue completion certificates to your learners. Learners see only their own certificates in Studio.

### Keep learners informed

Each course has **Updates** for announcements and **Forums** for discussion among the course community.

## Enrollment status

Every enrollment has one of three statuses:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Active | The learner has access to the course. |
| Completed | Reserved for course completion, which is planned. Today enrollments are Active or Revoked. |
| Revoked | Access was withdrawn, for example after a full refund. |

A revoked enrollment is final. If the learner joins the course again, for example by buying it again, they get a new enrollment.
