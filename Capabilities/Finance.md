# Finance

*The financial truth of your business — every value event, recorded once and kept in balance.*

Finance is where money becomes record. A sale, a payment, a refund, a subscription renewal — each becomes a balanced, auditable entry in a governed double-entry ledger. Because Finance shares the platform's one business model, the rest of the Suite posts into it automatically: commerce records a sale, and Finance is where it becomes permanent.

## What you can do

- **Keep an audit-grade ledger** — a double-entry general ledger where every entry balances and posted entries are immutable.
- **Bill and invoice** — turn orders into invoices, capture payments and receipts, and track what's owed.
- **Work in any currency** — record in the transaction currency, hold balances per account, and report in a common one.
- **Fiscalize and comply** — fiscal periods, tax policies, and standards-ready foreign-currency treatment.
- **Recur revenue** — manage subscription plans, renewals, and the licenses and entitlements customers hold.
