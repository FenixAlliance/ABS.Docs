# Operations

*Run the work — from a purchase order to a shipped product, tracked end to end.*

Operations is how your business gets things done: sourcing, making, moving, and delivering. Inventory, warehouses, production, purchasing, projects, and quality all share one model, so a stock movement, a work order, and a project task are part of the same connected picture — and every movement of value posts into [Finance](~/Capabilities/Finance.md).

## What you can do

- **Manage inventory** — items, warehouses, stock levels, and movement, with the visibility to reorder at the right time.
- **Make things** — production planning, work orders, and workstations for manufacturing.
- **Source and procure** — purchase requests, suppliers, and requisition-to-order flows.
- **Deliver projects** — projects, tasks, time tracking, and delivery.
- **Assure quality** — inspections, checks, and audits.
- **Track assets** — fixed-asset registers, transfers, and lifecycle.
- **Automate the flow** — model repeatable processes and approvals with visual workflows.
