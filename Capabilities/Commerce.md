# Commerce

*Sell anywhere — online, in-store, and everywhere in between — from one catalog.*

Commerce is how you sell. It unifies your catalog, pricing, and orders across every channel — a white-label online store, a point-of-sale register, a quote from your sales team — so back-office, storefront, and floor all read and write the same data. A sale made in any channel flows straight into [Finance](~/Capabilities/Finance.md) and [Operations](~/Capabilities/Operations.md).

## What you can do

- **Run an online store** — a customizable, white-label storefront with catalog, categories, and sales channels.
- **Manage products and pricing** — items, variants, price lists, promotions, and multi-currency pricing.
- **Sell in person** — point-of-sale checkout and register operations that share the same catalog and inventory.
- **Move deals through the pipeline** — quotes, orders, and invoices, with carts, wishlists, and comparisons for shoppers.
- **Reach more channels** — connect marketplaces and storefronts through official integrations.

## Modules that deliver it

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Commerce](~/Modules/eCommerce.md) | Online store — catalog, categories, carts, and sales channels |
| [Sales Hub](~/Modules/Sales-Hub.md) | Quotes, orders, invoices, price lists, and loyalty |

Point-of-sale checkout also lives in this family. Built on the **[Alliance Business Model (ABM)](~/Capabilities/Alliance-Business-Model.md)** foundation, and integrated end to end with [Finance](~/Capabilities/Finance.md), [Operations](~/Capabilities/Operations.md), and [Customer Experience](~/Capabilities/Customer-Experience.md).
