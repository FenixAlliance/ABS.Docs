# Customer Experience

*Know your customers, serve them well, and reach them where they are.*

Customer Experience is every touchpoint with the people you serve — the relationships you keep, the support you provide, the campaigns you run, and the sites and communities where you meet them. It all revolves around one shared contact record, so a lead, a shopper, a support case, and a newsletter subscriber are the same person, seen whole.

## What you can do

- **Manage relationships** — a single source of truth for contacts and organizations, with profiles that let one contact play many roles.
- **Support customers** — tickets, a knowledge base, returns, refunds, and warranties.
- **Market and engage** — campaigns, email and newsletters, social posts, and lead capture.
- **Build community** — member profiles, posts, reactions, and real-time chat.
- **Publish content** — build external-facing websites and portals where audiences sign in, browse, and transact.
