# Trust & Compliance

*Prove it — turn documents and actions into tamper-evident, non-repudiable records you and your regulators can rely on.*

Trust is **not** the same as identity. [Identity & Access](~/Capabilities/Identity-and-Access.md) proves who's signed in right now; **Trust proves that a document or action is authentic, hasn't been altered, and can be verified by an outside party long after the fact** — even by someone who was never logged into your system. It's the capability behind e-signatures, certificates, and the sealed evidence that makes a record legally meaningful.

## What you can do

- **Sign and seal documents** with legally-meaningful e-signatures — signing requests, certificates, and signature representations.
- **Collect trust evidence** — a tamper-evident record of who signed what, when, and how, kept for non-repudiation.
- **Let external parties sign** through a dedicated portal, without needing a Studio account.
- **Keep sealed, append-only records** — once an artifact is sealed it can't be altered, so you can prove it wasn't.
- **Report on ESG and compliance** — the sustainability and governance metrics your stakeholders ask for.
