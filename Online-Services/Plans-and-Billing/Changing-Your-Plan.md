# Changing your plan online

> **Coming.** Changing to a paid plan online, and paying for it online, is not available yet. This page describes how it will work. Today, contact support and we change the plan for you.

You will be able to move your workspace to another plan yourself. You see the exact price first, and nothing changes until you confirm it.

> If your workspace shows a "talk to us" note instead of the plan list, online plan changes are not switched on for it yet. Contact support and we change the plan for you.

## Who can change the plan

- A person signed in as themselves, with permission to create subscriptions in the workspace. Agents and automations cannot change a plan.
- The workspace needs exactly one active paid plan. If it only has the Free plan, buying a paid plan is a first purchase, not a change.

## Step 1: see the price

1. Open **Workspace > Configuration > Licensing > Subscriptions** and choose to add a plan.
2. Pick the plan and billing cycle you want, and select **See the price**.

The quote shows:

- the price of the new plan for its first period;
- the credit for the unused time of the period you already paid;
- the amount due now;
- the new billing period.

Prices are shown in your workspace's currency. Conversion uses current exchange rates. If no current rate is available, the price is not shown, and you can try again later.

## Step 2: confirm

Select **Confirm the change**. The button shows the amount due now.

- **Something to pay:** you go to the pay page. The new plan starts when the payment is received.
- **Nothing to pay:** when your credit covers the whole price, nothing is charged. The remaining credit becomes extra time on the new plan.

Your current plan keeps working until the new one starts. Renewals of the new plan are at its full price.

A quote is valid for 5 minutes. If the price has changed since you saw it, you are asked to see the price again before you confirm.

## How the credit is worked out

- The credit is what you actually paid for the current period, times the share of that period still left, counted to the second and rounded down.
- A period whose invoice is unpaid, or has a credit note, gives no credit.
- A paid period can be credited only once.
- The credit is never paid out as a refund. When it is larger than the new price, it lengthens your first period on the new plan, up to 3 years. A longer period goes to our team for review.

## Limits

- One plan change per workspace every 24 hours.
- You can change between plans of the same family from the same seller.
- To move to the Free plan, cancel your paid plan instead. It ends at the end of the period you paid.
- The old and new plan must be priced in the same currency.
- If a payment arrives after your current period has already ended, the change is not applied. Contact support.
