# AI credit packs

> **Coming.** Prepaid AI credit packs are built and switched off in 2.7.0. This page describes how they will work. We will announce when they are on.

A workspace on a paid plan will be able to buy prepaid AI credits in packs.

## The packs

| Pack | AI usage it adds | Price |
|---|---|---|
| AI credits 10 | USD 10 | USD 13.00 |
| AI credits 25 | USD 25 | USD 32.50 |
| AI credits 50 | USD 50 | USD 65.00 |

Packs are priced in US dollars. If your workspace pays in Colombian pesos (COP), the price is converted at the exchange rate of the moment you get the quote, and that same rate is used on the invoice. Packs can't be bought in other currencies yet.

## Buying a pack

1. Open **Workspace > Configuration > Licensing > Usage** and choose a pack.
2. Confirm the purchase. You get an invoice from Alliance Business Suite, with one line for the pack, in USD or COP.
3. Pay the invoice. The credits are added to your workspace once the invoice is paid.

Who can buy:

- a person signed in as themselves, with permission to create subscriptions in the workspace (agents and automations cannot buy credits);
- only on a paid plan.

## Your credits

- Credits expire 12 months after the payment.
- When you have credits from more than one pack, the ones that expire soonest are used first.
- Your balance never goes below zero.

## Refunds

- If the invoice is annulled before any of its credits were used, all of them are taken back.
- A partial credit note takes back a matching share of the credits.
