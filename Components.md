# Components

ABS is a **modular, layered platform**. Beneath the [capabilities](/The-Platform.md) you adopt sit five **platform foundations** — the enduring concepts that make everything cohere as one product. Each builds on the foundations beneath it: the Shared Kernel at the base, the operational cockpit at the top. The foundations are *concepts*; the components named here are how they're realized.

- **The Shared Kernel — [Alliance Core Libraries (ACL)](/Components/Alliance-Core-Libraries.md).** The platform's common technical language: the abstractions, contracts, and standards everything else depends on, plus the single curated source of third-party dependencies.
- **The Business Language — [Alliance Business Model (ABM)](/Components/Alliance-Business-Model.md).** The canonical, declarative model of standard business entities — the shared vocabulary that lets Accounting, CRM, Commerce, HR, Workflow, Marketplace, and Analytics coexist.
- **Trust — [Alliance Passport Service (APS)](/Components/Alliance-Passport-Service.md).** Identity, authentication, authorization, federation, security, and governance for every contact — customer, employee, partner, or guest.
- **Integration — [Alliance Business Platform (ABP)](/Components/Alliance-Business-Platform.md).** The multi-protocol surface — REST, GraphQL, gRPC, MCP, SignalR — for transacting with the model from any client or language.
- **Operations — [Alliance Business Studio](/Components/Alliance-Business-Studio.md).** The cockpit where organizations manage their implementation, transact data, generate views and reports, and customize and extend the system.

The mobile experience lives in **[Alliance Business Pocket](/Components/Alliance-Business-Pocket.md)**.

Each foundation has held through every generation of ABS, even as its implementation matured — see **[The Platform](/The-Platform.md)** for the full story and **[Advanced](/Advanced.md)** for the dependency tree. What you *build with* and *adopt* are the capabilities on top; these foundations are why they work as one.
